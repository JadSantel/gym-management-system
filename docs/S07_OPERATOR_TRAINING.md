Local synthetic demonstration only (FR-13). In Members, create the reception registration link and download its QR. The link expires after 30 days; replacing it immediately invalidates old printed codes. Use Open registration form on the same computer for local testing. A phone cannot reach another computer's loopback address; public access needs a separately verified reachable HTTPS deployment and current readiness gates.

Visitors enter synthetic name/email/Philippine mobile. Saved profiles appear as Pending; contacts are unverified and no message is sent. Do not treat a profile or contact as proof of identity, payment or access.

Open the profile and use the existing approved plan/full-cash review. Collect full cash before confirmation; short payment grants nothing. Retrieve the receipt and recheck current eligibility. Task 2 separately adds QR issuance/attendance/corrections after the GitHub checkpoint; staff will remain responsible for confirming the person presenting a personal QR. No real training or physical-device rehearsal is established by these instructions.

## Supported work

1. Find or create a member, review the approved plan, exact full cash amount, and proposed dates.
2. Collect the entire amount in cash before confirming. A partial amount or digital claim does not create a payment or paid period.
3. If a cash confirmation response is unknown, retry the saved operation with its original key. Do not collect again until its result is known.
4. Retrieve the durable receipt from the member profile or payment history. Check eligibility from the effective paid period; a future scheduled period is not active early.
5. Read historical payment and operational records as retained context. Historical digital methods, cases, adjustments/refunds, outage visits/custody, and closing snapshots are read-only and do not change current eligibility by themselves.

## Retired workflows

Digital collection/verification, Exceptions (investigation, omitted-payment recovery, correction and refund), outage receipt/custody/provisional access, and daily closing are retired. Their user-facing pages are removed; former APIs return HTTP 410. Historical records and links remain preserved.

This guidance defines no emergency process outside the CRM. If the application is unavailable, do not infer authority to issue paper receipts, grant provisional access, record digital claims, or perform a closing. The gym owner needs a separate operational decision for any such process.
